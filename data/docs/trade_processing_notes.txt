Operations Notes - Trade Processing

Common issues seen after trade capture:

1. Duplicate trade received from an upstream feed.
2. Incorrect settlement date caused by calendar configuration.
3. Missing counterparty settlement instruction.
4. Security identifier does not match the security master.
5. Quantity or price differs from the execution source.

For duplicate trades, compare source reference, counterparty, instrument, quantity and execution timestamp before cancelling or suppressing a record.
